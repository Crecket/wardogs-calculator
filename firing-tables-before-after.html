Firing tables: before and after

What the 2026-09-02 firing-range measurements changed. Before is the shipped vacuum fit; after is the quadratic-drag model fitted to the shots. Black dots are the measured shots themselves — the ground truth both lines are trying to hit. Every dot and every RMS below uses that shot's own real ΔZ, resolved from Bakurani's terrain, rather than assuming level ground.

Updated 2026-09-03, second session. Thirty-one further shots at deliberately varied heights added the dial floor (35 and 100 mil) and 440/540 mil to the low arc. Two findings do not fit on a chart, so they are stated here. The fit's error changes sign at 100 mil — the shell outruns the model by 153 m at 35 mil and the model outruns the shell by about 100 m at 150–200 mil — which no two-parameter drag fit can reproduce, so retuning muzzle velocity and drag cannot repair it. And the gun's own attitude is worth up to 95 m on the high arc: identical dials fired from a 35–50° hillside fall 47–121 m short of the same dials fired from level ground. The high arc is therefore not charted against the second session's shots, because those measure the hull's pitch rather than the shell's flight.
